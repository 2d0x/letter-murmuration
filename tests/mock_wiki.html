Section 1

Starlings are small, noisy birds that spend winter evenings in enormous flocks. Before settling for the night they wheel above the roost for half an hour, and every bird keeps an eye on only a handful of neighbours. Yet the whole cloud bends, splits and rejoins as if it were one animal; people call this a murmuration.[1] Starlings are small, noisy birds that spend winter evenings in enormous flocks.
