Letter Murmuration — bookmarklet

A bookmarks-bar button that turns every letter of the open page into a flock of starlings or a horde of zombies. Install it once, then it's one click on any page.

English · Русский

How to install

  1. Show the bookmarks bar: Ctrl + Shift + B, on a Mac — ⌘ + Shift + B.
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar:
    Murmuration

    No need to click it here — just drag it.

  3. Open any Wikipedia article and click the bookmark. A panel appears in the bottom right: 2 — flock, 3 — horde, 1 — letters settle back. To turn it off, press Esc or click the bookmark again.

Won't drag?

Copy the code, then right-click the bookmarks bar → “Add page…”, name it “Murmuration” and paste the code into the URL field.

The whole script lives inside the bookmark, __SIZE__ KB: nothing is downloaded and nothing is sent anywhere.

This whole page will take off — the bookmark works the same way on any site.

LXXIII

That time of year thou mayst in me behold
When yellow leaves, or none, or few, do hang
Upon those boughs which shake against the cold,
Bare ruined choirs, where late the sweet birds sang.
In me thou see’st the twilight of such day
As after sunset fadeth in the west,
Which by and by black night doth take away,
Death’s second self, that seals up all in rest.
In me thou see’st the glowing of such fire
That on the ashes of his youth doth lie,
As the death-bed whereon it must expire,
Consumed with that which it was nourished by.
  This thou perceiv’st, which makes thy love more strong,
  To love that well which thou must leave ere long.

  • Works in Chrome, Edge and Yandex Browser on desktop.
  • Sites with strict protection (Google, YouTube, GitHub) may block the script — then the bookmark simply does nothing.
  • When a new version comes out, drag the button again: the old bookmark keeps working as before.
